# Kanojo Studio — Open Questions

_Topics that need decisions before or shortly after launch._

---

## 1. Content Policy

Must be completed before public launch.

- What content is permitted / not permitted (explicitness, minors, real identities)
- How violations are detected and enforced (automated vs. manual review)
- Responsibility when users upload a face that isn't their own
- Regional compliance requirements

---

## 2. Look Curation Pipeline

The quality of the Look catalog determines the quality of the entire product. Who creates Looks, at what volume, and how quality is maintained:

- **Phase 1** — In-house curated only: full quality control, limited catalog
- **Phase 2** — Selected creator program: vetted contributors with editorial review
- **Phase 3** — Open submission: community-created Looks with approval gate

Questions:
- How many Looks are needed at launch to feel like a real catalog?
- What's the visual quality bar? Who sets and enforces it?
- Are Looks versioned / updatable after publish?

---

## 3. First Shoot (Onboarding)

A new user must reach their first beautiful portrait in under 60 seconds — before they encounter the full complexity of Looks, Moods, Scene Worlds, and Vibe Transfer.

Questions:
- What is the minimum viable input for a first shoot? (Face only? Face + one Look?)
- Does the system suggest a starting Look automatically?
- Is there a guided "first shoot" flow separate from the main Studio?
- What happens if no face is uploaded — does the system assign a default face silently or prompt the user to choose?

The first successful shoot is the moment a visitor becomes a user.
